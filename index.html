概览 Overview

关于我 About Me

我是一个在 2011 年秋天出生、正在长大的普通学生。小学六年在北京度过，现在是六力学校的初中生。 我喜欢什么、擅长什么、想去哪里，这些答案还在慢慢成形——所以这份记录不着急写完， 只把每一段真实发生过的事留下来。 An ordinary student born in the autumn of 2011, still growing up. Six years of primary school in Beijing, now at Liuli School. What I love, what I am good at, and where I want to go are still taking shape — so this record does not rush. It simply keeps what has really happened.
